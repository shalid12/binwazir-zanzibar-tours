Ruaha National Park Safari

Ruaha National park is believed to have high concentration of elephants than any national park in East Africa. It is also a place where, magnificent mammals like Kudu (both Greater and Lesser), Sable and Roan antelopes can easily be spotted in Miombo woodland. The male Kudu have beautiful spiraled horns while male Sable antelope have impressive curved horns. The park is also a habitat for endangered wild dogs. Other animals in the park include, Giraffe, Lions, Zebras, Leopards, Impala, Eland, Kudu, Black Antelope, Baboons, Wildebeests, Buffaloes, Cheetah, wild dog, bat, eared foxes, Jackals and hyenas- and it’s known for its Ruaha’s dry river beds also the curious act of elephants digging for water and you cannot miss the wonderful rock formations and endemic birdlife making Ruaha so very unique. Following its recent expansion, Ruaha is currently East Africa’s biggest national park and the second-largest on the African continent. It is also Tanzania’s most sizeable elephant haven and home to a profusion of bird species, including kingfishers, plovers, egrets, hornbills and sunbirds. Is the only protected area in which the flora and fauna of eastern and southern Africa overlap, leading to fascinating combinations of wildlife! Crocodiles and hippos thrive in the Great Ruaha River, which flows along the sanctuary’s eastern border and in the dry season between July and November, animals flock to the last remaining water sources, presenting an exceptional wildlife spectacle.

Your journey

Day-by-day itinerary

3 days / 2 nights
1

Day 1: Dar Es Salaam to Ruaha National Park

Depart by scheduled flight from Dar es Salaam to Ruaha National park landing at Msembe/Jongomero Airstrip depending on the Camp you shall be accommodated. Arrival at the Airstrip you will be met by driver guide and transfer to your camp/lodge for hot lunch while enjoying en-route game viewing. You will enjoy your hot lunch at the Lodge/Camp and followed with Afternoon Game drive in Ruaha National Park and return to the Lodge /Camp for Dinner and overnight.

2

Day 2: Full day Game Drive in Ruaha National Park

On this day you will have early breakfast, followed by a full day game drive with picnic lunch or 2 half-day games drives returning to your camp/lodge for hot lunch. In the evenings after game drive you will return to the camp/lodge to enjoy the bonfire, delicious meals under the stars and overnight.

3

Day 3: Departure

After an early breakfast, enjoy a final morning game drive in Ruaha National Park before arriving at the Ruaha airstrip where you will catch your scheduled flight back to Dar es Salaam for your flight back home.
