Tour overview

Discover Game and Local Fishing

The Game and Local Fishing Tour with Binwazir Zanzibar Tours and Safari offers a unique blend of wildlife adventure and cultural immersion. Experience the thrill of game viewing in nearby reserves, where you can spot a variety of animals in their natural habitat. Complement this with an authentic local fishing experience, learning traditional fishing techniques from the island's fishermen.

This tour provides an excellent opportunity to connect with nature and local communities, offering insights into the daily lives and traditions of Zanzibar's coastal people. Enjoy scenic landscapes, fresh seafood, and the warm hospitality of the locals during this memorable half-day tour.

Experience highlights

  • Enjoy a private experience with a flexible pace
  • Discover Zanzibar’s nature, culture and local stories
