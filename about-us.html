Who we are

A local team with a passion for meaningful travel

Binwazir Zanzibar Tours and Safari is a locally based travel company in Stone Town. We help guests experience Zanzibar’s history, beaches, food and communities, as well as the remarkable wildlife of mainland Tanzania.

Our approach is simple: listen carefully, plan responsibly and make every guest feel looked after. Whether you have a few hours or a complete holiday, we shape the experience around your interests, pace and travel style.

  • Private, flexible planning
  • Knowledgeable local guides
  • Clear communication
  • Carefully selected partners
