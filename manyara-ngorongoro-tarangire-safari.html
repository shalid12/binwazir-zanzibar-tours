Safari overview

Lake Manyara, Ngorongoro & Tarangire

exploring the northern Tanzania but still wants to see the big 5 and enjoy the nature, The safari takes you through Lake Manyara National Park; Ngorongoro Crater and Tarangire National Park our aim is to offer you a short but intense Northern Circuit Safari experience, for a period of 3 days / 2 nights to experience the game excitement of viewing in three of the most spectacular national game parks in Tanzania.

Safari highlights

  • Private safari vehicle and professional guide
  • Carefully planned park routing
  • Accommodation options for different budgets
  • Support with transfers and connections
Your journey

Day-by-day itinerary

3 days / 2 nights
1

Day 1: Arusha – Lake Manyara

Pick up from Arusha Airport with our professional tours guide/driver who will give you safari info briefing, after which, you will travel to Lake Manyara National Park for a Half Day Game Drive after game drive you will Arrive at the Lodge for Dinner and overnight.

2

Day 2: Ngorongoro Crater

After breakfast you will depart the lodge with packed Lunch to the crater for a Full-day Game Drive, Lunch will take place at the picnic site at the crater floor. Arrive at the Lodge for Dinner and overnight

3

Day 3: Tarangire National Park – Arusha

After early breakfast our driver will transfer you to Tarangire National Park for a Half day game drive. After Game drive in the late afternoon, then you have 1 hour drive to Arusha Airport via curio shops where you can grab some souvenirs to take back home with you or Zanzibar

A flexible private safariDaily timing and activity order may change because of flights, road conditions, weather, wildlife movement or accommodation availability.
