Safari overview

Tarangire, Lake Eyasi & Ngorongoro

This 4day tour offers an exhilarating exploration of two of Tanzania's renowned wildlife area: Tarangire, Ngorongoro Crater and Cultural experience from the hadzabes community. Each place boasts unique landscapes and abundant wildlife, promising an unforgettable safari and cultural experience. From the iconic baobab trees and elephant herds of Tarangire to the breathtaking scenery and high concentration of wildlife in the Ngorongoro Crater and amazing adventure with the Hadzabes. Take a journey into the “Gods must be crazy” movie. This tour gives you the chance to interact with the hadzabe community for hunting and cultural exchange. Hadzabe are an ethnic group in central Tanzania; last true nomads of Africa living around Lake Eyasi in the central Rift Valley and in the neighboring Serengeti Plateau. This tour provides an immersive adventure into the heart of Africa's wilderness.

Your journey

Day-by-day itinerary

4 days / 3 nights
1

Day 1: Arusha- TARANGIRE NATIONAL PARK

Early morning departure from Arusha to Tarangire National Park. Arrive at Tarangire National Park and embark on a game drive. Afternoon game drive exploring different areas of Tarangire, later drive to Karatu town for Dinner and overnight at a lodge.

2

Day 2: Karatu - Lake Eyasi

Early morning after breakfast; a brief introduction from our driver guide then start drive to lake eyasi arrives early at camp. You relax in your rooms and later spend the whole of the afternoon exploring the lake helped by the locals and getting to know hadzabe; are not closely related to any other people. While traditionally considered an East African branch of the Khoisan peoples, primarily because their language has clicks, modern genetic research suggests that they may be more closely related to the Pygmies. The Hadzabe language appears to be an isolate, unrelated to any other. During dry season, you walk through the dried lake and the bush. Return to your Camp for dinner and overnight.

3

Day 3: Full Day with hadzabe

After an early morning breakfast, you depart for your cultural tour with the hadza community. This is your day with typical cultural tribe and engages in real hunting together. It is a tribe of hunters and collectors, which formerly inhabited the Serengeti, but was forced away by stronger and more aggressive tribes. These are hunters and gatherers for honey and insect in the bush, men usually forage individually, and during the course of day usually feed themselves while foraging, and also bring home some honey, fruit, or wild game when available. Their huts are simple, built in a few hours and as easily abandoned. During this time, men often hunt in pairs, and spend entire nights lying in wait by waterholes, hoping to shoot animals that approach for a night-time drink, with bows and arrows treated with poison. The poison is made of the branches of the shrub Addendum coetaneous while women forage in larger parties, and usually bring home berries, baobab fruit, and tubers, depending on availability. Men and women also forage co-operatively for honey and fruit, and at least one adult male will usually accompany a group of foraging women. During the wet season, the diet is composed mostly of honey, some fruit, tubers, and occasional meat. Later return to your camp for dinner and overnight.

4

Day 4: Lake Eyasi – Ngorongoro Crater - Arusha

Early morning departure from Lake Eyasi to Ngorongoro Crater, 3hours drive to Ngorongoro Crater and check in for the half day game drive. After lunch, ascending the crater and depart for Arusha City. Evening, drop off to your hotel or proceed to Kilimanjaro International airport for flight home.
