Safari overview

Nyerere & Mikumi National Parks

Combine a Rufiji River boat safari with vehicle safaris in Nyerere and Mikumi, returning by train. The route can be tailored to your dates, group size, preferred accommodation and available connections.

Safari highlights

  • Private safari vehicle and professional guide
  • Carefully planned park routing
  • Accommodation options for different budgets
  • Support with transfers and connections
Your journey

Day-by-day itinerary

5 days / 4 nights
1

Day 1: Dar es Salaam → Nyerere National Park

- Early departure from Dar es Salaam - Arrival and check-in at Nyerere Tented Camp - Hot lunch at the camp - Afternoon boat safari on Rufiji River - Dinner and overnight at camp

2

Day 2: Full Day Game Drive – Nyerere National Park

- Early breakfast - Full-day game drive - Picnic lunch inside the park - Dinner and overnight at Nyerere Tented Camp

3

Day 3: Nyerere → Mikumi National Park

- Breakfast at the camp - Drive to Mikumi National Park - Arrival and check-in at Camp Bastian - Dinner and overnight

4

Day 4: Full Day Game Drive – Mikumi National Park

- Early breakfast - Full-day game drive with packed lunch - Dinner and overnight at Camp Bastian

5

Day 5: Mikumi → Morogoro → Dar es Salaam

- Early breakfast - Morning game drive en route - Drive to Morogoro town - SGR train back to Dar es Salaam

A flexible private safariDaily timing and activity order may change because of flights, road conditions, weather, wildlife movement or accommodation availability.
