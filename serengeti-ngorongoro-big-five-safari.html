Your journey

Day-by-day itinerary

4 days / 3 nights
1

Day 1: Arrival Day in Arusha City

On arrival time, our driver will pick up at Kilimanjaro International Airport or Arusha Airport and drive to the hotel in Arusha City.

2

Day 2: Arusha to Serengeti National park

Early Morning after breakfast, depart from the hotel with packed lunch box drive to the endless plain of Africa with its large number of the wildlife concentration. Our professional driver guide will explain to you the wonders of the Ngorongoro Conservation Area as you pass through the area. Check in at the park gate; on that time you will be able to go for viewing point located near to gate, where you will have good views of the wildlife, there are regular sightings of Grant gazelle, Thomson gazelle, Impalas, and other bovid species like buffaloes and elands. Late morning proceed to the hotel before starting the game drive. After to check in at the hotel, the game driver will start; majority of the animals tend to be seen within the park due to its structure. You can see lion, leopard, cheetah, hippos inside the pool but sometime may be seen outside the pool, at the part of migration zebra, wildebeest; gazelle may be seen in a huge herds, giraffe, duiker, dik dik, kudu, Oryx, monkeys, baboons, elephants, and Caracal. You will take a break for lunch at the picnic site there after lunch continues with the game drive until the evening. Evening is a wonderful time to spot the animals resting under the shadow tree. Late evening en route back to the lodge for dinner and overnight.

3

Day 3: Full game drive in Serengeti National Park

Early morning before breakfast, depart from the Camp for the early morning game drive. The early morning game drive has its uniqueness; most of the nights active animals they can be easy seen at this time such as Lion, leopard and other nocturne animals. After the morning game drive will be back to the lodge; appetizing breakfast will be served after there the full game drive will start with lunch boxes in your vehicle. We will take the scenic route inside the Serengeti. Let’s explore the huge number of animals from the savannah grasslands, acacia forests and savannah woodland. Serengeti is arguably the best know wildlife sanctuary in the world. It is the home of millions of large mammals, including what is so called the Big 5: African elephant, African lion, Cape buffalo, African leopard and the Black rhinoceros and over 500 bird’s species have recorded such as eagles, owls, starling, crown crane, vultures and secretary bird. It is home to the world Great animal’s migration; more than millions of wildebeest associated with zebra, gazelle and other opportunist like Hyena. There is a high possibility of spotting every animal you were expected, hippos in swamps. Late evening drive back to the Camp for dinner and overnight

4

Day 4: Serengeti – Ngorongoro Crater - Arusha

Depart from Serengeti after early breakfast and drive to Ngorongoro Crater with your lunch box. The Ngorongoro Conservation Area, which has been declared a world heritage site, home of up to 30,000 large mammals, more than 100 species of birds and it’s the world’s largest caldera and it is the place easy to find big cats and clans of hyena not to forget rhino and hippos in the swamps. The Ngorongoro Crater lies within Ngorongoro Conservation Area; the crater is well known for its uniqueness which comprises the crater animals, lake within a crater and the view. You will descend to the crater and spot the crater animals such rhino, hyena, jackals then visit the swamps to see the rhino then drive towards the exit gate while enjoying the nature beauty of the Ngorongoro area en-route to Arusha then Airport for the flight.
