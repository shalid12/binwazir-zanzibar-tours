Booking & payments

How do I book a tour or safari?
Contact us through WhatsApp, email or the contact page with your preferred dates, number of travelers and interests. We will confirm availability, explain the itinerary and provide the booking details.
How far in advance should I book?
We recommend booking as early as possible, especially during busy holiday periods. Short-notice requests are welcome and will be arranged when guides, vehicles and required reservations are available.
Are your trips private or shared?
Many experiences can be arranged privately for your group. If a shared option is available, we will identify it clearly before you confirm.
What payment methods do you accept?
Available payment methods and any deposit requirement are confirmed with your quotation. Please do not send payment until our team has confirmed your booking details.
What is your cancellation policy?
Cancellation terms may vary because hotels, flights, park fees and third-party services have different conditions. Your applicable terms will be shared before confirmation. Contact us quickly if plans change so we can discuss the available options.

Zanzibar tours

What is normally included in a Zanzibar tour?
Inclusions depend on the experience and may include a private guide, transport, entrance fees, boat services, meals or refreshments. The exact inclusions and exclusions are listed in your itinerary before booking.
Can you collect us from our hotel?
Hotel pickup and return can be arranged for most tours. The price may depend on your hotel location, so please share the hotel name when requesting a quotation.
Can tours be customized?
Yes. We can adjust timing, pace and suitable combinations based on your interests. Some sites have fixed opening times, tides or conservation rules that may affect the final schedule.
Are the tours suitable for children?
Many tours are family-friendly. Tell us the children’s ages so we can recommend comfortable timing, suitable activities and any necessary safety arrangements.

Tanzania safaris

Can I take a safari while staying in Zanzibar?
Yes. Fly-in safaris from Zanzibar are popular and can range from a short park visit to a multi-day itinerary. Options depend on flight schedules, season, park choice and budget.
Which national park should I visit?
The best choice depends on your available time, wildlife interests, travel season and budget. We can explain the differences between destinations such as Mikumi, Selous/Nyerere, Tarangire, Serengeti and Ngorongoro.
Is safari accommodation included?
For multi-day safaris, accommodation can be included at the level agreed in your itinerary. Your quotation will clearly state the selected property, meals and other inclusions.
Do I need travel insurance?
We strongly recommend comprehensive travel insurance covering medical care, cancellation, baggage and the activities in your itinerary. Insurance is normally the traveler’s responsibility.

Transfers & logistics

Do you provide airport and hotel transfers?
Yes. We arrange private transfers between Zanzibar airport, ferry terminal, Stone Town and hotels across the island. Send your arrival details and destination for a quotation.
What happens if my flight or ferry is delayed?
Notify us as soon as possible through WhatsApp or phone. We will coordinate with the driver and advise you of any change to the pickup arrangement.
Where will I meet my guide?
The confirmed meeting location, pickup time and contact details will be shared before the experience. For hotel pickups, please be ready in the reception area at the agreed time.

Travel preparation

What should I bring on a Zanzibar tour?
Comfortable clothing, sun protection, drinking water, suitable footwear and a respectful cover-up for cultural or religious sites are useful. Swimwear and a towel may be needed for marine experiences.
What should I pack for safari?
Bring neutral lightweight clothing, a warm layer for early mornings, comfortable closed shoes, sun protection, binoculars if available and any personal medication. Luggage limits may apply on light aircraft.
Do I need a visa or vaccinations?
Entry, visa and health requirements depend on nationality, route and current regulations. Check official government and health guidance before travel; our team can help identify what you should verify.
How can I travel responsibly?
Respect local customs, ask before photographing people, reduce single-use plastic, follow wildlife and marine guidance, dress appropriately in communities and support local businesses where possible.
