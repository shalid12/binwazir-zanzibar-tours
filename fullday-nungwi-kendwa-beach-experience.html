Tour overview

Discover Nungwi & Kendwa Beach

The Full Day Nungwi and Kendwa Beach Experience with Binwazir Zanzibar Tours and Safari offers a perfect day of relaxation and exploration on two of Zanzibar’s most beautiful beaches. Enjoy the pristine white sands and crystal-clear turquoise waters of Nungwi and Kendwa, ideal for swimming, sunbathing, and water activities. This tour provides a tranquil escape where you can soak up the sun, take in stunning coastal views, and experience the vibrant beach culture of Zanzibar. It’s an ideal choice for those seeking a leisurely day by the ocean in a picturesque setting.
