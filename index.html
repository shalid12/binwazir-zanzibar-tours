Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to help you plan your Zanzibar experience with confidence.

How do I book a tour or safari?
Contact us through WhatsApp, email or our contact page with your preferred dates, group size and interests. We will confirm availability and send the booking details.
Can tours be customized?
Yes. We can adjust the timing, pace and combination of activities around your interests, hotel location and available time.
Can you collect us from our hotel?
Hotel pickup and return can be arranged for most experiences. Share your hotel name when requesting a quotation so we can confirm the arrangement.
Can I take a Tanzania safari from Zanzibar?
Yes. We arrange fly-in safaris from Zanzibar, from short wildlife escapes to multi-day itineraries based on your time and budget.
What should I bring on a Zanzibar tour?
Bring comfortable clothing, suitable footwear, sun protection and water. Swimwear, a towel or a respectful cover-up may be useful depending on the experience.
