Saadani National Park Safari

Saadani National Park Safari Saadani is where the beach meets the bush. The only wildlife sanctuary in East Africa to boast an Indian Ocean beachfront, it possesses all the attributes that make Tanzania’s tropical coastline and islands so popular with European sun-worshippers. Yet it is also the one place where those idle hours of sunbathing might be interrupted by an elephant strolling past, or a lion coming to drink at the nearby waterhole

Your journey

Day-by-day itinerary

3 days / 2 nights
1

Day 1: Dar es Salaam – Saadani National Park

Depart Dar es Salaam in the morning to Saadani National Park, on arrival you will meet the driver guide with packed lunch and you will proceed to enjoy your half day game drive within the park all the way to the beautiful sunset. After Game drive you will be taken to the Camp/lodge for Dinner and overnight in Saadani National Park.

2

Day 2: Full Day game Drive and boating

With the early morning sunrise you will engage in game viewing, followed by breakfast at the Lodge /Camp, at this time the elephants are relatively shy and usually hide during the day in the woodlands. You can seldom spot leopards in dense bushes and trees, as these animals are mainly nocturnal and can be found living in close proximity to humans. Other animals you can see in the park are the greater kudu and smaller antelopes such as suni and duiker. The trees are inhabited by colubus monkeys, adorable bush babies and many fruit eating bird species, insects and butterflies. After the rewarding game drive, you can engage in campfire. Dinner and overnight will be lodge or Camp in the park.

3

Day 3: Saadani to Dar es Salaam.

In the last day after breakfast, you will start your early morning boat safari, there is a chance you may spot hippos, crocs and many birds, and it’s interesting to watch the vegetation along the banks of the Wami River change with the decreasing salinity of the water as you move upstream. In some sections there are also marked variations between the two banks, with areas of date palms and lush foliage on one side, and whistling thorn acacias reminiscent of drier areas of the country on the other. Afternoon, enroute game drive back to Dar es Salaam for the flight back to your home country or Zanzibar
