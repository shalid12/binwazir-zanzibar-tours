Your journey

Day-by-day itinerary

5 days / 4 nights
1

Day 1: Moshi / Arusha – Tarangire National Park

Pick up at the hotel and drive from Moshi or Arusha to Tarangire National Park with packed lunch. Arrival late morning and check in at the lodge or Camp. Start game drive then late back to Lodge or Camp. Dinner and overnight at the lodge or Camp on full board

2

Day 2: Tarangire – Serengeti via Olduvai Gorge

Early morning breakfast before drive to Serengeti via Olduvai Gorge archeological site with picnic lunch. Enroute game drive to endless plain ‘’Serengeti’’. Dinner and overnight at Lodge or Tented Camp around Ndutu Area.

3

Day 3: Full day game drive in Serengeti National Park

Morning breakfast then start the full day game drive with picnic lunch and late evening back to the lodge or Camp on full board. Dinner and overnight at Lodge or Tented Camp

4

Day 4: Serengeti – Ngorongoro Crater

After breakfast check out at the Camp and drive from Serengeti to Ngorongoro. Arrival late morning and check in at the lodge or Camp on full board then half day crater tour. Dinner and overnight at Lodge or Tented Camp

5

Day 5: Ngorongoro – Lake Manyara – Arusha / Moshi

Early breakfast then drive to Lake Manyara National Park and check in at the park and start the game drive with picnic lunch. Late afternoon drive back to Moshi or Arusha or drop off at the airport.
