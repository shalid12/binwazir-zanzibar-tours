Safari overview

Serengeti & Balloon Safari Experience

Serengeti National park The Serengeti National park is the largest and most famous park in Tanzania, known worldwide for its endless plains, this Park is an arguably the best-known wildlife sanctuary in the world with over 3 million large mammals. About 35 species of plains animals can be seen there including the “big 5” Elephant, Rhino, Lion, Leopard and Buffalo. Other common species found here includes hippo, giraffe, eland, impala and other antelope types, baboons, monkey and a profusion of over 500 species of birds. It is home to the greatest wildlife spectacle on earth- the great migration of wildebeest and zebra. Serengeti is another good combination of the top famous national parks in Tanzania; this tour is guaranteed to blow your mind off with their unique wildlife and amazing sceneries.

Your journey

Day-by-day itinerary

3 days / 2 nights
1

Day 1: FLY FROM ZANZIBAR TO SERENGETI NATIONAL PARK

Depart from Zanzibar by your scheduled flight to Serengeti National park via Arusha (change planes in Arusha or direct flight). On arrival, you will meet the driver tour guide with packed lunch and proceed with afternoon game drive. Dinner and overnight at your booked Camp /Lodge in the heart of Serengeti

2

Day 2: BALLON SAFARI AND GAME DRIVE IN SERENGETI NATIONAL PARK

Early morning pick up at the Camp, around 5:00AM and drive to Balloon flying point around 6:30 AM it’s time for a balloon launch and the approximate return is 9:45 AM. After balloon safari and breakfast, depart for game drive to explore the Serengeti. Although predicting the movements of the famous wildebeest migration is not an exact science, you have a good chance of seeing this unforgettable spectacle at this time of year, Search for the ‘big five’ as well as the myriad smaller animals which make each safari unique. Returning to your Camp /Lodge for dinner and overnight.

3

Day 3: FLY FROM SERENGETI TO KILIMANJARO INTERNATIONAL AIRPORT

There will be a morning game drive en route to the airstrip. Connect morning scheduled flight back to Kilimanjaro International Airport back home
