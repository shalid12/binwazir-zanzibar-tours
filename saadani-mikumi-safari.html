Your journey

Day-by-day itinerary

5 days / 4 nights
1

Day 1: Dar - Saadani

Depart Dar es Salaam to Saadani National Park with lunch packed, sunset game viewing in the park. Saadani is where the beach meets the bush. The only wildlife sanctuary in East Africa to boast an Indian Ocean beachfront, it possesses all the attributes that make Tanzania’s tropical coastline and islands so popular with European sun-worshippers. Dinner and overnight will be at Camp/Lodge

2

Day 2: In Saadani

Early morning sunrise game viewing, back to the Camp/Lodge for breakfast. Safaris optional include walking safaris, game drives and boat safaris. Dinner and overnight will be at Lodge/Camp.

3

Day 3: Saadani - Mikumi

Morning after breakfast, depart to Mikumi National Park for sunset game viewing. Animals commonly found Include lion, eland, hartebeest, buffalo, wildebeest, giraffe, zebra and elephants. Wild dog can be seen in packs here. Dinner and overnight will be at Lodge/Camp.

4

Day 4: Full day game drive in Mikumi

Morning after breakfast, full day game viewing in the park. The Mikumi flood plain is the main feature of the Park along with the bordering mountain ranges. Animals commonly found here include lion, eland, hartebeest, buffalo, wildebeest, giraffe, zebra, hippo and elephant. The Mikumi elephants are mainly grazers and do not causes damage. Dinner and overnight will be at Camp/lodge

5

Day 5: Mikumi - Dar

Early morning sunrise game viewing, back to the Lodge/Camp for brunch. Late afternoon games drive en route back to Dar es Salaam for your flight or boat to Zanzibar.
