Safari overview

A complete Mikumi adventure in one day

Our one-day safari tours depart from Zanzibar and fly to Mikumi National Park. They are ideal for travelers with limited time who still want to experience Tanzania's incredible wildlife and breathtaking landscapes. Expert guides take you through the park for opportunities to see some of Africa's most iconic animals. The tour includes a picnic lunch and complimentary mineral water.

This condensed but exciting wildlife experience offers chances to spot lions, elephants, giraffes, zebras and many bird species.

Safari highlights

  • Round-trip flights between Zanzibar and Mikumi
  • Game drive in an open 4x4 safari vehicle
  • Lions, elephants, giraffes and zebras
  • More than 400 recorded bird species
  • Open plains, woodlands and wetland scenery
  • Picnic lunch and mineral water included
